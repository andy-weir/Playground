# Stakeholder Interview Guide

Semi-structured interview script for internal stakeholders (CS, Support, Sales, Onboarding). 30 minutes per session.

---

## Warm-Up (5 min)

- What's your role and how long have you been at Feathr?
- How often do you interact with customers directly?
- What types of customers do you work with most?

## Current Experience (10 min)

### Navigation & Findability

- When customers ask you for help, what are the most common tasks they're trying to accomplish?
- Where do customers get stuck most often in the app? Are there specific screens or sections?
- When customers describe what they're looking for, what words do they use?
  - Probe: Do they say "contacts," "people," "supporters," "audience," or something else?
  - Probe: Do they say "campaigns," "marketing," "ads," or something else?
  - Probe: How do they refer to the Community section?

### Feature Discovery

- Are there features you regularly have to point customers to because they can't find them?
  - Probe: Forms? Super Pixel? Segments? Integrations?
- When customers are trying to find settings, where do they typically look first?
- Have you noticed customers confusing any sections or features with each other?

## Deep Dive (10 min)

### Onboarding

- Walk me through a typical onboarding experience. Where do customers drop off?
- What do you think are the biggest barriers to a customer getting value from Feathr for the first time?
- If a customer's champion leaves, what happens? How do new contacts get re-onboarded?

### Terminology & Mental Models

- If you had to describe Feathr's main sections to a brand new user in plain language, how would you organize it?
- What features do you think should be easier to find?
- The current navigation uses "Community" for contacts — does that term resonate with customers?

### Partner Marketing

- How often do customers use Partner Marketing features?
- Do customers ever confuse Partner Marketing with regular marketing/campaigns?
- When you explain the difference, what language works best?

## Reaction (optional, if time — 5 min)

Show the proposed IA structure (top-level items only):
- Quick Start, Dashboard, Campaigns/Marketing, Partner Marketing, Fundraising, Audience, Content, Reports, Account Settings, Help

- What's your first reaction?
- Anything surprising about how things are grouped?
- Where would you expect to find [probe on RQ items]?

## Wrap-Up (5 min)

- Anything else about the app's navigation or organization that we should know about?
- If you could change one thing about how Feathr is organized, what would it be?

---

## Notes for Interviewer

- Record sessions (with permission) for later synthesis
- Watch for: specific terminology customers use, emotional reactions to complexity, stories about customer confusion
- Don't lead — if they say "People" instead of "Audience," note it but don't correct
- After each session, capture 3-5 key observations in `research/synthesis/raw/`
