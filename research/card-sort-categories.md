## Notes

- Cards are intentionally labeled with plain language, not Feathr-specific jargon
- "Super Pixel" is kept because that's the branded name users encounter
- "Campaign Settings" is a single card despite containing multiple sub-items — we want to see if users group settings with their parent feature or with other settings
- The card sort should be run BEFORE participants see the proposed IA prototype to avoid priming
